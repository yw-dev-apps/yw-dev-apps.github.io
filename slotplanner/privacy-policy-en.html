Privacy Policy

Slot Planner

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This app helps you plan and record your day as time slots on a timeline. The operator does not collect or sell your plans, tasks, or related content. This app does not include third-party advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

This app does not require an account. Plans, tasks, and settings are not sent to the operator’s own servers.

Content you enter or configure is stored on your device (and, when enabled, in your iCloud as described below) as follows:

DataPurposeWhere stored
Tasks, timeline plans, categories, completion records, etc.Display, editing, analytics in-app, backup/shareOn device (SQLite). When iCloud sync is on: your iCloud private database (CloudKit)
Display, timeline range, appearance, and similar settingsApp featuresOn device (e.g. UserDefaults)
Local notification lead times, etc.Reminders before start timesOn device

If you use backup, export, or the system share sheet, content is passed to apps you choose (Files, Mail, Messages, etc.). It does not go through the operator’s servers.

3. iCloud sync

This app may use Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to sync plans and tasks across devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

  • Synced data is stored in the private database of your iCloud account
  • The operator cannot access your iCloud data
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, or sync is turned off, data stays on that device only
  • You can turn sync on or off in the app settings

4. Calendar access

To overlay calendar events on the timeline, the app may ask for permission to read your calendars.

  • Event data is read on-device for display
  • Calendar data is not sent to the operator’s servers
  • You can change permission anytime in iOS Settings

5. HealthKit (sleep)

To show sleep bands on the timeline, the app may ask for permission to read sleep data from HealthKit.

  • Sleep data is read on-device for display
  • HealthKit data is not sent to the operator’s servers
  • You can change permission anytime in iOS Settings

6. Notifications

This app may show local notifications as reminders (for example before a plan starts). It does not use remote push notifications. You can change notification permission anytime in iOS Settings.

7. Shortcuts / App Intents

If you add tasks via Siri Shortcuts or App Intents, the content is saved as app data on your device (and in your iCloud when sync is enabled). It does not go through the operator’s servers.

8. Outbound data and tracking

  • This app does not send plans, tasks, or settings to the operator’s own servers
  • iCloud sync is provided by Apple
  • No third-party ad SDKs or operator analytics SDKs are used
  • The operator does not engage in tracking across other companies’ apps or websites of the kind that requires App Tracking Transparency

9. Apple Watch

The current App Store build does not include an Apple Watch app. If Watch features are offered later, this policy will be updated.

10. Deleting data

  • Individual plans and tasks can be deleted in the app
  • Deleting the app generally removes app data stored on that device
  • Data synced to iCloud may remain in your Apple ID’s iCloud storage. Manage iCloud data using Apple’s tools

11. Children

This app is not directed at a specific age group. If used by someone under 13, please do so with parental consent.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page and, if appropriate, notify you in the app.
